Welcome

A landmark church with an open door

Second Presbyterian is a National Historic Landmark on Michigan Avenue, home to nine Tiffany windows and a sanctuary built for worship. The building is remarkable. The people are the point.

We are a Reformed congregation in the Presbyterian Church (USA), and we welcome everyone: women and men, gay and straight, lifelong believers and the simply curious. Come as you are.

What We BelieveMeet Our Pastors

Whoever you are, wherever you are on your journey of faith, you are welcome here.
