Children are welcome here

At Second, little ones are part of the worshipping congregation, and we welcome their noise and their needs.

On Sunday morning

Kids belong in the room

We do not tuck children away. They worship right alongside the rest of us, and there is a children's message during the service just for them.

We keep books, coloring materials, and fidget toys on hand. And if you need a quieter moment, a cry room with an audio stream of the service is always open.

Bringing little ones for the first time? Come as you are. You will not be the only family in the pews, and you will not get any side-eye for the wiggles.

Bring the Whole Family

We would love to meet your family

Join us this Sunday, or reach out with any question about visiting with children.
