Sunday Worship

There's a place for you here.

Whether you're a weekly churchgoer, haven't been in ages, or don't know anything about Christianity, you are welcome at Second.

When we gather

11am

Every Sunday morning

1936 South Michigan Ave
Chicago, IL 60616

Get DirectionsWatch Online

Our service follows a traditional, liturgical format, with hymns, spirituals, and anthems led by organ and choir. We celebrate the Eucharist on the first Sunday of each month.

Come as you are. Casual attire is always welcome. There is no wrong way to be here, and no experience required.

Planning your first visit?

Here's what to know

Getting here

We are at 1936 South Michigan Ave in the South Loop, close to public transit, with first-come, first-served free parking.

What to wear

Whatever feels right to you. Some dress up, many do not. Come as you are.

For families

Children are welcome in worship, with a children's message and a cry room if you need it. More for kids.

Accessibility

The sanctuary is accessible. If you need any accommodation to join us, reach out and we will be glad to help.

Little ones welcome

Children belong in worship

Little ones are part of the worshipping congregation at Second, and we welcome their noise and their needs. We keep books, coloring materials, and fidget toys on hand, and there is a children's message during the service.

Need a quieter moment? A cry room with an audio stream of the service is always available.
